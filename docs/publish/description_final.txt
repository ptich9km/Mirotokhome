Mirotokhome is a procedurally generated sandbox RPG with tactical combat, inspired by the classic Allods II: the same idea of a living map, rebuilt from scratch with procedural generation.

IMPORTANT: the interface is in English by default. A full Russian translation (interface, items, dialogue, voice-over) is switchable in the main menu and the choice is remembered.

WHAT'S IN THE GAME

• Every world is generated from a seed: biome, cities, roads, mountains, water, faction outposts. One seed = one map, save it and come back.
• 12-sector astral map: travel between islands through portals at the map edge.
• Warrior or mage: four combat directions plus a secondary one, own spellbook.
• Tactical pause: the whole world stops and orders are given while paused.
• Party: mercenaries with orders (follow, guard, attack, hold).
• Combat: one damage formula for everything — elements, shields, resistances and "weapon vs creature type": a mace hits undead for 125% and humans for 75%.
• 10 grey monster species in 5 tiers, from "Fierce Wolf" to "Wolf Lord".
• Forge and workshop: an item goes +1…+7, a failure rolls back one step but the item survives.
• Factions: four races, reputation, three capturable outposts per map, rival tags.
• Herbalism and alchemy: 12 animated herbs in four tiers grow by zone and are gathered by hand; 16 recipes, including 7 tactical elixirs.
• Russian voice-over with a separate timbre per race: orcs distorted, undead breathy, druids higher and quieter.
• Fog of war, minimap, and a random "save the traveller" encounter: Grey monsters beat a peaceful NPC, rescue him for gold and reputation with the nearest city.
• Mods: a folder with mod.json, add a spell school, an item or a monster — data only, no engine changes.

NOT IN YET (honestly)

• Story campaign — sandbox and procedural quests only.
• Multiplayer.
• Polished balance: numbers change after every play session.
• Full English interface is in and on by default; switch to Russian in the main menu.

This is an alpha published to find bugs and learn what people want to play. If something breaks, tell me what you did and what happened — that is enough.

CONTROLS

LMB — move / attack, 1-3 — spells, Space — tactical pause, M — astral map, Esc — menu. Keys can be rebound in the settings.

SAVES

6 slots plus autosave, stored in user:// (the game folder in AppData). The map is rebuilt from its seed on load.

MODS

Mod folder: %APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Mirotokhome\mods — drop a folder with mod.json there and restart the game.

MADE WITH GODOT 4.7
