ПОЛЕ: AI generation disclosure
----------------------------------------------------------------
Yes. Character and monster sprites, faction item art, ground loot art and some
voice lines were generated with AI image/voice models, then cropped, recoloured
and normalized by project scripts and hand-tuned. Tier recolours are programmatic.
Code, design, balance, writing and game data are made by the author with AI
assistance as a tool. Sound effects and music are CC0 library assets, not AI.
